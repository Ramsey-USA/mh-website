# Quality Management Guide

**Category:** Project - Quality Management  
**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026  
**Status:** Active

## Quick Summary

This guide captures the current markdown baseline for MH quality-management controls.

Use it when updating inspection, turnover, nonconformance, or first-work quality language so those changes stay aligned with the controlled 01 Core Doctrine source set.

## Numbered Ecosystem Source

- `documents/input/01-core-doctrine/mh-quality-management-plan-v1-0-draft.docx`

## Purpose and Authority

The quality-management doctrine establishes the accountable process, required evidence, and release conditions for the MH quality program. It does not authorize departure from contracts, law, technical instruction, manufacturer requirements, or safety expectations.

## Required Controls

1. Project quality plan and inspection/test plan.
2. First-work inspection, hold points, and mockups.
3. Nonconformance reporting, corrective action, and turnover verification.

## Operating Workflow

1. Approve the project quality and inspection/test planning package before mobilization.
2. Stop at designated hold points until required acceptance evidence is complete.
3. Issue a nonconformance record when work does not meet requirements.
4. Reinspect and verify closure before releasing nonconforming work.

## Evidence and Records

The doctrine source identifies the following proof-of-execution examples:

- `QMP-PLAN-ITP-001`
- `QMP-NCR-001`
- Closeout turnover index

Records should capture the applicable project or transaction reference, preparer, date/time, reviewer, disposition, system-of-record location, and closure evidence.

## Escalation and Assurance

Exceptions require written scope, reason, risk, compensating control, owner, approval authority, expiration, and evidence location.

Material quality failures, overdue actions, repeat events, suspected fraud, safety risks, cyber events, or contract exposures escalate immediately to the accountable owner and executive leadership.

## Documentation Rule

When quality language changes, update related delivery, closeout, subcontractor-performance, and turnover references in the same change so process guidance and evidence rules stay synchronized.
