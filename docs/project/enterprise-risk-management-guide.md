**Category:** Project - Enterprise Risk

## Quick Summary

This guide captures the current markdown-facing baseline for MH enterprise risk management.

Use it when updating project, contract, insurance, cash, cyber, or reputation-risk language so those updates stay aligned with the controlled 01 Core Doctrine source set.

## Purpose and Authority

The current enterprise risk doctrine establishes accountable process, required evidence, and release conditions for risk management. It does not authorize departure from contracts, law, technical instruction, or safety requirements.

Owner-assigned delegates may execute the process, but accountability for performance and evidence quality remains with leadership.

## Required Controls

1. Contract review and obligation ownership.
2. Insurance, bonding, and key-person continuity.
3. Cash, supply-chain, cyber, and reputational exposure tracking.

## Operating Workflow

1. Record the risk, cause, consequence, likelihood, impact, and accountable owner.
2. Assign mitigation action, due date, escalation trigger, and evidence location.
3. Review material risks quarterly and after any material event.
4. Escalate immediately when a material failure, fraud concern, cyber event, safety issue, quality failure, or contract exposure is identified.

## Evidence and Records

The doctrine source identifies the following records as current proof-of-execution examples:

- `SAL-RISK-CONTRACT-001` contract risk review
- Enterprise risk register
- Executive management-review record

Each record should identify the applicable project, employee, asset, vendor, or transaction reference; preparer; date/time; reviewer; disposition; system-of-record location; and closure evidence where applicable.

## Bonding and Surety Relationship

The bonding addendum treats the surety relationship as enterprise-critical. Public-facing documentation should keep bonding language factual and controlled rather than promotional.

Current controlled themes from the bonding addendum:

- Payment and performance bond requirements must be satisfied before mobilization when required by contract or governing law.
- Quarterly WIP schedules and year-end audited financials support aggregate bonding capacity governance.
- Any notice of claim against an MH payment bond escalates immediately to executive leadership.

## Documentation Rule

When risk or bonding language changes, update the related doctrine, project, safety, and business references in the same change so risk posture, trust language, and evidence expectations do not drift.
