## Core Brand Anchors

- **Company:** MH Construction, Inc.
- **Founded:** 2010
- **Veteran-Owned Since:** January 2025
- **Primary slogan:** “Built on Quality, Backed by Trust.”
- **Tagline:** “Founded 2010, Veteran-Owned Since January 2025”
- **Core values:** Honesty, Integrity, Professionalism, Thoroughness

## Messaging Rules

- Lead with construction terminology first.
- Keep the voice relationship-first, factual, and free of hype.
- Preserve veteran-owned framing as context, not as the primary SEO label.
- Use supporting lines consistently and avoid off-brand alternatives.

## Visual Rules

- Use the approved MH font family, Mendl Sans Dusk.
- Use the approved color system: Hunter Green, Leather Tan, Secondary Text, and Architectural Bronze.
- Keep component and layout choices aligned with the unified component standards.
