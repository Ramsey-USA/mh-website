# MHC Intellectual Property and Public Disclosure Control

Status: Draft  
Authority: MH Ecosystem  
CHENG and Chief Editor: Matt Ramsey  
Final approval: Jeremy Thamert, Owner and CEO

## Command intent

This control protects MHC-owned brand assets, prevents MH Construction from claiming intellectual property owned by an affiliated sister company, and limits public disclosure of proprietary procedures. It is an operational safeguard, not a substitute for advice from qualified intellectual-property counsel.

## Source of truth

The machine-readable register is `mhc-ip-register.json`. Public website content, documents, campaign assets, quotations, and software references must follow that register.

## CHENG flags

| Flag             | Release effect                                                                               |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `CHENG-IP-RED`   | Stop expansion or new publication until clearance and ownership are recorded.                |
| `CHENG-IP-AMBER` | Freeze legacy use; assemble first-use, authorship, assignment, license, and source evidence. |
| `CHENG-IP-BLUE`  | Present the asset to CHENG as a registration candidate after clearance.                      |
| `CHENG-IP-BLACK` | Keep internal, restricted, or attributed to its external owner.                              |
| `CHENG-IP-GREEN` | Permit only the uses, symbols, pages, and media recorded in the register.                    |

## Current command decisions

### Built on Quality, Backed by Trust

- Existing use may remain while clearance is performed.
- Do not create variants, add `SM`, or use `®` without a recorded CHENG decision.
- Assemble dated specimens, first-use records, original source files, and authorship history.

### MISH Safety & Health Program

- Existing use may remain under its full construction and regulatory context.
- Preserve OSHA, WISHA, APP, SSSP, and other required regulatory terminology.
- Do not add a trademark symbol until acronym, expanded-name, authorship, and market clearance are complete.
- Public content may explain the program and verified outcomes; internal procedures, decision matrices, and project controls stay controlled.

### Words From the General

- Freeze the current library. Do not add new quotations until the written approval record is established.
- Jeremy Thamert must approve the series name, quotation attribution, and final wording presented as his direct speech.
- Matt Ramsey remains Chief Editor; editing history and human authorship must remain traceable.
- No trademark symbol may be used before clearance and a CHENG decision.

### Affiliated software platforms

BidPilot, Build Pilot, Marketing Flight, and Field Command Center are developed under an unnamed sister company by the Chief Navigation Officer with CHENG operational influence. MH Construction does not claim ownership.

Public MHC routes must use the descriptor `secure affiliated systems` unless a written cross-company license and naming approval authorize a specific platform reference. Technical architecture, source code, operating logic, and commercial positioning remain outside the public MHC website.

### Adobe licensed production assets

Adobe Creative Cloud Pro is an approved production source, subject to the active account and each asset's specific license.

- Use Adobe Fonts on the website only through an Adobe-hosted Web Project embed unless MHC obtains a separate self-hosting license from the foundry.
- Keep approved fallback fonts in the website font stack and document a replacement route for subscription continuity.
- Record every production font, icon, Stock asset, and template in the licensed-asset ledger before publication.
- The record must include the Adobe asset or project ID, source creator or foundry, license class, license date, permitted channels, MHC project, custodian, and evidence location.
- Exclude assets marked `Editorial Use Only` from commercial MHC marketing.
- Do not claim copyright, trademark ownership, or exclusivity in the underlying Adobe asset.
- Keep raw licensed files out of public repositories unless the recorded license expressly permits distribution.

## Publication rule

Publish capability, verified evidence, and business outcomes. Do not publish the underlying operating recipe.

Public content may include:

- verified construction capabilities and markets;
- approved project evidence;
- substantiated licenses, awards, safety statistics, and veteran-owned claims;
- approved leadership statements;
- approved document excerpts and controlled downloads.

Public content must exclude:

- proprietary approval thresholds and decision matrices;
- internal role succession, security architecture, credentials, or vulnerabilities;
- dashboard logic, database structures, barcode strategies, or software source code;
- personnel, medical, financial, incident, or disciplinary records;
- third-party logos or protected assets without recorded authorization.

## Release gate

The website pipeline must fail when:

1. an affiliated platform name appears in a public application source;
2. `®` is attached to an uncleared MHC candidate;
3. the primary slogan, MISH, or Words From the General lacks its governing register entry;
4. the quote library lacks its legacy-freeze and approval-pending metadata;
5. public copy states or implies that MH Construction owns the affiliated platforms;
6. the register loses named authority, lifecycle status, publication decision, or next action.

## Clearance package

For each candidate, CHENG will eventually need:

- exact mark and intended use;
- owner and human-author record;
- first-use anywhere and first-use-in-commerce evidence;
- federal, state, common-law, domain, and market search results;
- goods and services description;
- specimens and source files;
- assignment, release, or license documents;
- AI-assistance disclosure where applicable;
- counsel recommendation;
- final executive decision.
