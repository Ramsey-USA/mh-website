# LinkedIn Engagement Operating Standard

## Funnel

`Matthew personal authority -> useful construction discussion -> contextual company proof -> exact website destination -> qualified action`

Authority precedes conversion. Personal posts do not default to sales language, and MHC companion posts do not duplicate Matthew's copy.

## Destination Control

Use this order: project page, case study, capability page, article or resource, service page, company overview, then homepage. Route leadership and professional-development subjects to `matthew-jon-ramsey.com`, general contracting to `mhc-gc.com`, and specialty drywall subjects to `hd-drywall.net`.

## Deterministic Post Identifier

Format: `{pillar}-{topic}-{evidence}`

Rules:

- Lowercase ASCII and kebab case only.
- Three durable segments.
- Maximum 64 characters.
- Use a project or resource slug for the evidence segment.
- Do not use names, email addresses, dates alone, or transient campaign language.
- Freeze the identifier after approval so personal and company content can be joined in reporting.

Campaign 001 identifier: `operations-active-site-sequencing-volm`

## Approval Workflow

1. Codex prepares the post pair, asset, links, release notes, and response options.
2. Matthew verifies personal voice and factual interpretation.
3. MHC verifies project facts, image rights, confidentiality, and company copy.
4. Matthew or the Page administrator publishes manually.
5. The owner records the live post URLs and publication time.
6. Analytics are captured at 24 hours, 72 hours, and seven days.
7. The next content decision uses qualified traffic and lead signals, not reactions alone.

## Comment Control

- Reply to the actual point raised.
- Add operational value before mentioning a company.
- Use a link only when it answers the question.
- Do not mass-reply, copy replies between members, or automate personal conversation.
- Escalate project-specific, contractual, personnel, safety-incident, and legal questions to the responsible MHC authority.

## Lead Signals

Track only legitimate first-party or authorized LinkedIn data. High-value signals include repeated engagement by owners, developers, GCs, architects, estimators, superintendents, qualified trade partners, direct capability questions, project questions, tracked website visits, phone clicks, and completed contact actions.

Do not scrape member data or create personal dossiers.

## Four-Week Operating Framework

| Week | Matthew personal posts | MHC companion | Primary destination |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Active-site sequencing; scope handoff discipline | Volm active-site case study | Volm project case study |
| 2 | Change visibility before field impact; leadership decision thresholds | No forced companion unless supporting proof is approved | Matthew site or MHC FAQ/resource |
| 3 | Pre-task planning as a production control; superintendent feedback loop | Verified safety-system evidence | MHC safety resource |
| 4 | Subcontractor coordination and long-lead risk; monthly lessons learned | Procurement and trade partnership capability | MHC procurement service |

Target two personal posts and one MHC post per week only when the content meets the proof and approval gate. HD content remains selective and topic-driven.
