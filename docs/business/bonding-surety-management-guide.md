## Quick Summary

This guide provides the current markdown-facing baseline for MH bonding and surety management language.

Use it when updating trust, compliance, prequalification, or insurer-facing business documentation so claims remain controlled and source-backed.

## Controlled Position

The bonding addendum treats bond default as enterprise-critical and requires active protection of the surety relationship.

This markdown guide should keep bonding language:

- factual
- non-promotional
- contract-aware
- synchronized with current risk and financial-control doctrine

## Current Controlled Themes

1. Payment and performance bonds must be secured before mobilization when required by governing contract or law.
2. Leadership maintains the surety relationship through current financial reporting, including quarterly WIP schedules and year-end audited financials.
3. Any notice of claim against an MH payment bond escalates immediately to executive leadership.
4. Deviations from protocol require formal written approval before execution.

## Documentation Rule

Do not turn internal bonding controls into unsupported public claims. Keep public-facing trust language qualitative unless the supporting documentary source, approval path, and audience usage are explicitly confirmed.

## Approved External Language Pattern

Preferred public-facing pattern:

- licensed in Washington, Oregon, and Idaho
- insurance and bonding requirements managed against current contract obligations
- project-specific compliance records and proof documents handled through controlled review paths

Avoid unsupported shorthand such as:

- blanket bonding-capacity claims without an approved evidence source
- universal insurance-limit claims presented as permanent marketing facts
- generic “fully covered” wording that bypasses contract-specific review requirements
