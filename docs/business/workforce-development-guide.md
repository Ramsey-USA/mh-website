## Purpose and Authority

The Workforce Development Matrix establishes the accountable process, required evidence, and release conditions for workforce training and competency management.

It supports the enterprise management system and should be read as an operating control surface rather than a recruiting or culture-only document.

## Required Controls

1. Role competency, course, and credential requirements.
2. Credential expiration, verification, and assignment restrictions.
3. Performance development and recurring training.

## Operating Workflow

1. Assign mandatory training by role and jurisdiction.
2. Verify required cards and qualifications before assignment to controlled work.
3. Track expirations, refresher timing, and supporting credential evidence.
4. Preserve the training record as controlled operating evidence.

## Handbook and Orientation Alignment

The workforce-control baseline should stay synchronized with the current handbook and onboarding surfaces.

Primary handbook-alignment points:

- `Introduction & Company Overview` -> first-day onboarding expectations
- `Employment Classifications & Schedules` -> performance review and role-expectation alignment
- `Health, Safety, & Security` -> training, safety orientation, and controlled-work readiness expectations
- `Technology & Data Use` -> onboarding requirements that depend on system-access and acceptable-use instruction
- `Disciplinary Action & Separation` -> performance improvement, review, and follow-up evidence

Current handbook form links that support workforce controls:

- `HANDBOOK-FORM-19` First-Day Onboarding Checklist
- `HANDBOOK-FORM-20` Employee Performance Review
- `HANDBOOK-FORM-15` Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

The new-employee orientation source under the 05 IT and Infrastructure family should remain aligned with these same expectations so onboarding, access, and training workflows do not drift between categories.

## Evidence and Records

The doctrine source identifies these proof-of-execution examples:

- Training matrix
- Operator certification log
- Toolbox talk attendance record

Each record should identify the applicable employee, project, preparer, date/time, reviewer, disposition, system-of-record location, and closure evidence where applicable.
