## Purpose

The document-control standard establishes command, editorial, revision, approval, system-boundary, and archival rules for the MH ecosystem.

The revision-history block template provides the standardized front-matter pattern for recording controlled changes across Tier 1 through Tier 5 core manuals.

## Current Lifecycle and Version Baseline

- Previously existing Employee Handbook, MISH, TBT, and SDS documents use Version `3.0` during Draft and Review.
- All other rebuilt or newly created ecosystem documents use Version `1.0`.
- Movement from Draft to Review does not change the version.
- Minor approved changes move to `.1` increments such as `1.1` or `3.1`.
- Major-number changes require formal authorization.

## Editorial and Approval Gate

- Matt Ramsey: Author / Chief Editor
- Todd Shoeff: Co-Editor
- Jeremy Thamert: Final Approver

Logical subject-matter reviewers are added by domain. Editorial review completes before movement from Draft to Review, and final approval requires both signature and secure dashboard evidence.

## Production Release Gate

- `CHENG`: brand, website, safety, stakeholder, and public-claim impacts
- `CHNAV`: sales, estimating, preconstruction, workflow, and usability impacts
- `IT Specialist`: security, integrations, backup, testing, deployment, and rollback
- `COO`: final production approval
- `CEO`: material policy, financial, public, enterprise-risk, and unresolved objections

## Records and Retention Baseline

- Audit records remain active in dashboards for one year and are then archived permanently on the in-house server.
- Supervisors may not delete controlled records; corrections create a traceable entry.
- Safety, personnel, CDL, financial, incident, insurance, title, maintenance, and disposition records follow longer governing retention schedules.
