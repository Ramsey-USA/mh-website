# KPI and Control Impact Statement - Packet 0002

## KPI impacts

Closeout SOP impacts:

- Closeout completeness KPI is now tied to mandatory artifact set.
- Pending digital sign-off KPI is now a release-gate indicator.
- Closeout cycle-time KPI gains clearer start/end boundary.

SAGE100 SOP impacts:

- Posting accuracy KPI gains explicit authority and reconciliation controls.
- Estimate-to-actual variance KPI gains CPCO collaboration checkpoint.
- AP/AR and exception-aging metrics are now review-trigger inputs.

Internal Server SOP impacts:

- Backup success KPI is tied to fixed cadence and same-day failure response.
- Restore-test pass KPI and MTTR KPI are now operationally owned and auditable.
- Continuity severity incidents become formal escalation-driven metrics.

## Control impacts

- Mandatory gate controls are now explicit for closeout release and financial closure.
- Continuity controls move from ad hoc practice to documented command protocol.
- Evidence traceability is strengthened across Procore, SAGE100, and server logs.

## Monitoring cadence impact

- Monday operations meeting receives sharper exception summaries.
- Quarterly audit package now includes continuity controls and closeout compliance trends.
