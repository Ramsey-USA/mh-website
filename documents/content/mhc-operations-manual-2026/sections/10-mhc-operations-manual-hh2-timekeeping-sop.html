Status: Draft v0.1 Command Owner: COO (XO) Backup Owner: HR Primary Reviewer: PM Lead Final Approver: CEO (CO)

1. Mission Purpose

Standardize HH2 employee sign-in and timekeeping controls so payroll and project labor records are accurate, auditable, and command-reliable.

2. Scope and Authority

System scope:

  • HH2 employee sign-in and time records

Authority model:

  • Employee entry responsibility: each employee
  • Field oversight responsibility: PM/PE and Superintendent support
  • Operational accountability: XO
  • Final command escalation: CO

3. Required Inputs

  • Active employee roster
  • Project and cost-code assignments
  • Approved work schedules
  • Exception reports (missed sign-ins, corrections)

4. Required Outputs

  • Complete HH2 daily time entries
  • Approved correction logs
  • Payroll-ready timekeeping data
  • Weekly exception summary for command review

5. SOP Steps

Step 1 - Daily Sign-In Execution

  • Employees sign in and out through HH2 according to assignment.
  • Entries must include proper project/cost-code context where required.

Step 2 - First-Level Review

  • PM/PE review entries for completeness and obvious anomalies.
  • Missing entries are flagged same day when possible.

Step 3 - Exception Handling

  • Correction requests must include reason and supervisor acknowledgment.
  • Unauthorized alteration attempts are treated as policy violations.

Step 4 - Payroll Handoff Control

  • Approved time data is prepared for payroll processing.
  • Exceptions are resolved or documented with command visibility before payroll close.

Step 5 - Reporting and Review

  • Weekly exception status reported in Monday 9:00 AM meeting when active issues exist.
  • Quarterly controls audit reviews exception patterns and compliance rates.

6. Controls and Thresholds

  • No payroll release from unreviewed HH2 data.
  • All corrections require documented reason and approver.
  • Repeated entry failures trigger supervisory intervention.
  • Intentional misuse of time records triggers disciplinary review.

7. KPI and Review Cadence

Weekly:

  • Missing sign-in rate
  • Correction request volume
  • Time-entry closure lag

Quarterly:

  • Timekeeping compliance rate
  • Repeat exception rate by project/team
  • Payroll-ready accuracy score

8. Evidence and Record Location

Primary evidence:

  • HH2 entry logs and correction records

Supporting evidence:

  • Supervisor review records
  • Payroll handoff summaries
  • Quarterly compliance audit artifacts

9. Escalation Path

Standard path:

  • Employee -> Supervisor/PM/PE -> XO -> CO

Exception path:

  • CHENG may escalate compliance risk directly to CO where safety or legal exposure intersects workforce tracking obligations.

10. Non-Compliance Response

  • First breach: correction and coaching.
  • Repeat breach: documented warning and retraining.
  • Severe breach: command review and disciplinary action per policy.
