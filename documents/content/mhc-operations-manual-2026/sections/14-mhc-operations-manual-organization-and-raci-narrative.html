1. Mission Purpose

Document how command authority, formal reporting, and operational support interact so teams can execute mission objectives without ambiguity.

2. Command Mapping (Navy Analog)

  • CO: CEO
  • XO: COO
  • Master Chief: Chief Superintendent (Reagan)
  • CHENG: Safety Officer/CMO
  • CPCO: Chief Preconstruction Officer (acting role currently Todd)

3. Structural Doctrine

Formal command doctrine:

  • Office and project management command largely routes through XO.
  • Master Chief leads field execution discipline.
  • CHENG and CPCO have domain command authority and exception escalation rights.

Operational support doctrine:

  • PMs and PEs directly support Master Chief in day-to-day field mission execution.
  • PMs and PEs formally report in XO command chain.

4. Role Succession Rule

If a role is unfilled, command duties roll to the immediate higher command level until relieved.

Current explicit application:

  • CFO role remains vacant; CFO command duties are carried by XO.

5. RACI Application Principles

  • One accountable owner per process.
  • Responsible roles execute and evidence the work.
  • Consulted roles provide required domain input before release.
  • Informed roles receive updates on material decisions and escalations.

6. Process-Family Ownership Snapshot

  • Command doctrine and release governance: XO accountable
  • Safety and compliance controls: CHENG accountable
  • Preconstruction estimating controls: CPCO responsible, XO accountable
  • Project execution and closeout controls: PM accountable, PE responsible
  • Financial controls and SAGE100 governance: XO accountable
  • Internal server continuity controls: IT Specialist responsible, XO accountable

7. Escalation Doctrine

Standard path:

  • Process owner -> XO -> CO

Exception rights:

  • CHENG may escalate directly to CO for mission-critical safety/compliance exposure.
  • CPCO may escalate directly to CO for mission-critical strategic preconstruction exposure.

8. Cadence and Governance

  • Weekly Monday 9:00 AM command-control meeting is mandatory.
  • Quarterly controls audits are mandatory.
  • Unresolved high-severity items remain active until command closure is documented.

9. Evidence and Traceability

  • RACI matrix appendix is source of detailed role assignment.
  • Approval authority matrix appendix is source of threshold and signing rights.
  • Weekly meeting records and quarterly audit packages are required evidence.
