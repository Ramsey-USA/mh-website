1. Purpose

This chapter defines command and control for estimating and preconstruction functions as the estimator team grows.

2. Role Designation

Working title for implementation:

  • CPCO: Chief Preconstruction Officer (Estimator Officer)

Meaning:

  • The CPCO leads preconstruction estimation command, cost navigation, and bid-discipline controls across estimating staff.
  • The CPCO role is parallel in command weight to CHENG for domain governance and mission-critical escalation.

Current assignment:

  • Todd serves as acting CPCO until the role is formally staffed.

3. Command Placement

  • Formal command chain: CPCO reports to XO.
  • Mission exception: CPCO may escalate directly to CO when material risk, strategic bid exposure, legal exposure, or mission continuity requires direct command visibility.
  • Estimating team members report through CPCO once the estimator unit scales.

4. Core Responsibilities

  • Lead estimating standards, scope assumptions, and risk qualification controls.
  • Maintain estimate package quality before PM handoff.
  • Coordinate bid strategy with XO, PMs, and PEs.
  • Validate preconstruction assumptions against field realities and schedule constraints.
  • Maintain SAGE100-facing cost coding congruency for Job Cost and purchasing controls.

5. Control Gates

Pre-bid controls:

  • Scope completeness check
  • Bid assumptions and exclusions register
  • Risk and contingency rationale
  • Subcontractor/vendor quote validation

Pre-award controls:

  • Estimate variance review against historical data
  • Margin and risk review by XO
  • CO notification when strategic or exceptional exposure exists

Handoff controls:

  • Estimating package transfer to PM/PE with approved baseline
  • Handoff memo includes assumptions, alternates, and risk flags

6. Cadence and Reporting

  • CPCO provides weekly update in Monday 9:00 AM operations meeting.
  • CPCO maintains an active bid pipeline status board and forecast view.
  • CPCO contributes to quarterly controls audit for estimate-to-actual variance analysis.

7. Metrics (Initial)

  • Bid-hit ratio
  • Estimate-to-actual variance percentage
  • Quote coverage completeness
  • Time-to-bid cycle
  • Post-award change exposure attributable to estimating assumptions

8. Interim Rule While Team Is Small

Until estimator staffing scales, Todd executes CPCO responsibilities directly while still collaborating with XO, CHENG, PMs, and PEs under existing command doctrine.
