Status: Draft v0.1 Command Owner: COO (XO) Backup Owner: HR Primary Reviewer: IT Specialist and Admin Assistant Final Approver: CEO (CO)

1. Mission Purpose

Maintain secure, reliable, and role-aligned business communications in Outlook/Microsoft 365 through standardized account lifecycle, access controls, and operational escalation pathways.

2. Scope and Authority

Platform scope:

  • Outlook and Microsoft 365 business communications

Authority model:

  • Provisioning and deprovisioning execution: IT Specialist and Admin Assistant
  • Operational control and approval: XO
  • Final command escalation: CO

3. Required Inputs

  • Approved role request (new hire, role change, termination)
  • Manager-approved mailbox/access request
  • Shared-mailbox membership request when applicable
  • Access exception rationale (if elevated rights are needed)

4. Required Outputs

  • Provisioned or deprovisioned mailbox account
  • Updated access roster and group membership log
  • Shared-mailbox delegation records
  • Retention and hold settings aligned with policy

5. SOP Steps

Step 1 - Account Provisioning

  • Create account using standardized naming and role metadata.
  • Assign baseline license and security controls.
  • Configure required mailbox groups and distribution lists.

Step 2 - Access Governance

  • Apply least-privilege mailbox access.
  • Route elevated-access requests to XO for approval.
  • Log delegated and shared-mailbox assignments.

Step 3 - Role Change Handling

  • Update mailbox access and group rights based on new role.
  • Remove obsolete rights immediately.
  • Confirm ownership continuity for project-critical communications.

Step 4 - Offboarding and Deprovisioning

  • Disable direct access on separation trigger.
  • Transfer business-critical mailbox content to approved owner.
  • Apply retention and archival controls per policy.

Step 5 - Operational Monitoring and Review

  • Validate weekly that pending account requests are current.
  • Flag unresolved access exceptions for Monday control meeting review.

6. Controls and Thresholds

  • No mailbox provisioning without approved request source.
  • No elevated access without XO authorization.
  • Separation-related deprovisioning is same-day priority.
  • Communication records retention follows conservative legal-first schedule.

7. KPI and Review Cadence

Weekly:

  • Provisioning and deprovisioning cycle time
  • Open access exception count
  • Shared mailbox ownership exceptions

Quarterly audit:

  • Access compliance score
  • Deprovisioning timeliness compliance
  • Retention settings compliance

8. Evidence and Record Location

Primary evidence:

  • Microsoft 365 admin logs and mailbox change records

Supporting evidence:

  • Access approval records
  • Delegation and shared-mailbox logs
  • Quarterly audit output

9. Escalation Path

Standard path:

  • IT Specialist/Admin Assistant -> XO -> CO

Exception path:

  • CHENG may escalate communications-compliance risk directly to CO when business continuity, legal, or safety obligations are affected.

10. Non-Compliance Response

  • First breach: corrective action and owner assignment.
  • Repeat breach: XO-led review and role-specific controls retraining.
  • Critical breach: CO notification and temporary access freeze authority.
