Status: Draft v0.1 Command Owner: COO (XO) Backup Owner: Chief Superintendent (Master Chief) Primary Reviewer: Safety Officer/CMO (CHENG) Final Approver: CEO (CO)

1. Mission Purpose

Establish a command-standard KPI system and reporting rhythm that gives leadership timely, accurate, and actionable visibility across safety, schedule, quality, cost, and closeout performance.

2. Command Ownership and Authority

  • KPI governance owner: XO
  • Domain KPI owners: CHENG, CPCO, PMs, PEs, Master Chief
  • Data integrity responsibility: process owners in each SOP chapter
  • Final command escalation: CO

3. Reporting Cadence

Mandatory rhythm:

  • Weekly operations meeting every Monday at 9:00 AM
  • Monthly controls packet to XO
  • Quarterly executive audit rollup to CO

4. Required Inputs

  • Procore logs and status reports
  • SAGE100 reconciliation and variance reports
  • HH2 timekeeping exception metrics
  • Safety and compliance logs
  • Internal server continuity metrics
  • Outlook access/compliance control logs

5. Required Outputs

  • Weekly command dashboard snapshot
  • Monthly controls package with trends and exceptions
  • Quarterly executive scorecard with corrective action status

6. KPI Families

Safety and compliance:

  • Incident count
  • Near-miss count
  • Corrective-action closure rate
  • Time-to-close critical findings

Schedule and execution:

  • Planned vs actual milestone reliability
  • Open RFI and submittal aging
  • Punchlist aging

Cost and financial controls:

  • Change-order cycle time
  • Threshold compliance rate
  • Estimate-to-actual variance
  • SAGE100 posting accuracy

Closeout and turnover:

  • Closeout package completeness rate
  • Pending digital sign-off count
  • Closeout cycle-time trend

Operations continuity:

  • Backup success rate
  • Restore-test pass rate
  • Critical incident mean time to resolve

7. Weekly Meeting Reporting Protocol

  • KPI owners submit updates before Monday meeting.
  • Meeting chair (XO) validates mission-impact exceptions first.
  • Open corrective actions are reviewed before new discretionary work.
  • Escalations are logged with owner and due date.

8. Controls and Thresholds

  • No KPI may be reported without source evidence.
  • KPI definition changes require XO approval.
  • Material data-quality defects must be corrected before executive rollup.
  • Repeated missing KPI submissions trigger command review.

9. Evidence and Record Location

Primary records:

  • Weekly meeting minutes and dashboard snapshots
  • Monthly controls packets
  • Quarterly executive rollup files

Supporting records:

  • System-generated exports from Procore, SAGE100, HH2, and control appendices

10. Escalation Path

Standard path:

  • KPI owner -> XO -> CO

Exception path:

  • CHENG may escalate directly to CO for mission-critical safety/compliance exposure.
  • CPCO may escalate directly to CO for mission-critical preconstruction risk exposure.

11. Non-Compliance Response

  • First breach: corrective coaching and immediate data correction.
  • Repeat breach: XO-led control review and role retraining.
  • Critical breach: CO notification with command remediation plan.
