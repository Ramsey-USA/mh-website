This workflow protects both win strategy and delivery quality. A proposal should not only be turned in on time. It should leave a clean record of what was offered, why it was priced that way, and how the relationship should continue after submission. When any follow-up may be reused externally, defer to [Proof and Approval Parameters](../marketing/parameters/proof-and-approval-parameters.md) before release.

  1. Confirm the due date, submission format, review path, and owner responsibilities.
  2. Gather the latest documents, addenda, stakeholder notes, and open questions.
  3. Align the estimator lead, leadership review, and specialty inputs before finalization.
  4. Deliver the proposal on time and with clear clarifications.
  5. Record the outcome, capture lessons, and maintain the relationship after submission.
  6. If follow-up language, recap assets, or owner-approved updates may be repurposed externally, align them with the shared parameters baselines before release.
