MHC uses a multi-channel media distribution stack so every project update has a clear job: build visibility, reinforce proof, or deepen trusted relationships.

The core stack includes:

  • Radio Ads and Commercials for market awareness and top-of-funnel brand recognition.
  • YouTube for long-form proof assets, walkthroughs, interviews, and recap content.
  • Website as the central hub for case studies, project storytelling, and SEO-aligned trust content.
  • LinkedIn for B2B authority, owner-facing updates, and professional proof.
  • Facebook for community visibility, culture, and Trade Partner recognition.
  • Google Business Profile for local search visibility, short-form trust updates, service-area-facing proof, and review-response discipline using the approved GBP guide and templates.
  • Instagram and X for visual proof, short updates, and milestone amplification when approved.
  • Email Blasts for direct communication with stakeholders, referral networks, and long-tail follow-up.

Each channel should publish only what it can support well. The goal is not volume. The goal is clear, evidence-backed communication that reinforces MH as a trusted construction partner.
