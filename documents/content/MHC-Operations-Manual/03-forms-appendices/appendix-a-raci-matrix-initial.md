# Appendix A - Initial RACI Matrix (Draft)

Status: Draft v0.1
Purpose: Establish accountability boundaries for operations-manual v1.

Legend:

- R = Responsible
- A = Accountable
- C = Consulted
- I = Informed

## Command Roles

- CO: CEO
- XO: COO
- Master Chief: Chief Superintendent (Reagan)
- CHENG: Safety Officer/CMO
- CPCO: Chief Preconstruction Officer (Estimator Officer; acting role currently Todd)
- PM: Project Manager
- PE: Project Engineer
- HR: Human Resources
- IT: IT Specialist

## Notes

- PM and PE formally report in XO chain while directly supporting Master Chief mission execution.
- CHENG may escalate directly to CO for mission-critical safety/compliance risk.
- CPCO may escalate directly to CO for strategic bid, legal, or mission-critical preconstruction risk.
- CFO responsibilities are currently assumed by XO until role is filled.
