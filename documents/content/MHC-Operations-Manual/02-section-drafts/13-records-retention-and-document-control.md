# MHC Operations Manual - Records Retention and Document Control

Status: Draft v0.1
Command Owner: COO (XO)
Backup Owner: HR
Primary Reviewer: Safety Officer/CMO (CHENG)
Final Approver: CEO (CO)

## 1. Mission Purpose

Set one internal records-retention and document-control standard that preserves legal readiness, operational traceability, and command accountability.

## 2. Scope

This chapter governs internal records across:

- Project controls and delivery artifacts
- Financial and accounting records
- Timekeeping and payroll records
- HR records
- Safety and compliance records
- Manual revision and policy records

## 3. Retention Baseline (Conservative)

| Record Type                                         | Minimum Retention         |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Project communication records                       | 7 years                   |
| Contracts, change orders, warranty records          | 10 years                  |
| Safety incidents, corrective actions, audit records | 10 years                  |
| Financial/accounting records (SAGE100 outputs)      | 7 years                   |
| Payroll and timekeeping records (HH2 related)       | 7 years                   |
| HR personnel records after separation               | 7 years                   |
| Training/certification records                      | Employment term + 5 years |
| Manual revision history                             | Permanent archive         |

## 4. Document Control Standards

- All controlled documents require version identifier and effective date.
- Draft, review, and approved states must be clearly marked.
- Approved records must be immutable except through formal revision process.
- Repository location and owner must be recorded for each controlled artifact.

## 5. Revision Workflow

Standard review order:

- CHENG review
- HR review
- XO approval
- CO final approval

Emergency update rule:

- XO may issue emergency policy patch with immediate CO notification.
- Emergency updates require post-implementation review at next control meeting.

## 6. Access and Legal Hold

- Access is role-based and least-privilege.
- Elevated access changes require XO approval.
- Legal hold directives suspend deletion/destruction for in-scope records until released by command direction.

## 7. Archival and Disposal

- Records are archived at close of active use phase.
- End-of-retention disposal must be approved and logged.
- Disposal logs must include record type, date, approver, and method.

## 8. Evidence and Record Location

Primary evidence:

- Version history logs
- Approval records
- Archive and disposal logs

Supporting evidence:

- Quarterly audit reports
- Exception and legal-hold records

## 9. Escalation Path

Standard path:

- Record owner -> HR/XO -> CO

Exception path:

- CHENG may escalate directly to CO for compliance-critical exposure.

## 10. Non-Compliance Response

- First breach: corrective action and process remediation.
- Repeat breach: XO-led governance review and retraining.
- Critical breach: immediate CO notification with hold and containment actions.
