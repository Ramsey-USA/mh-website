# MHC Operations Manual - Project Execution and Closeout SOP

Status: Draft v0.1
Command Owner: Project Manager (PM)
Backup Owner: COO (XO)
Primary Reviewer: Chief Superintendent (Master Chief)
Final Approver: COO (XO)

## 1. Mission Purpose

Standardize project closeout execution so each project is released with complete documentation, validated training records, and mandatory Procore digital sign-off.

## 2. Command Ownership and Authority

- Closeout assembly owner: PE
- Closeout quality and release owner: PM
- Field completion validation: Master Chief and Superintendent
- Command escalation authority: XO
- Final command escalation authority: CO

Command note:

- Closeout flow is PE -> PM for standard release.
- XO intervenes when package quality, risk, or compliance exceptions are unresolved.

## 3. Required Inputs

- Contract closeout requirements
- Final approved change-order log
- Final as-built documentation from subcontractors and MHC
- Equipment and systems turnover data
- Warranty documents and operations references

## 4. Required Outputs

Mandatory closeout package includes:

- O&M documents
- Performance warranty
- Manufacturer warranty
- As-builts (subcontractor and MHC sets)
- Equipment training records and handoff confirmation

Release outputs:

- Procore digital closeout sign-off record
- Final closeout transmittal package
- Internal archive packet and controls evidence

## 5. SOP Steps

### Step 1 - Closeout Kickoff (PM + PE)

- PM opens closeout workstream and target release date.
- PE issues closeout document request list to responsible contributors.
- Master Chief validates field completion priorities that affect closeout.

### Step 2 - Package Assembly (PE)

- PE compiles all required closeout artifacts.
- PE verifies naming, revision status, and package completeness.
- PE logs missing items and due dates by responsible party.

### Step 3 - Package Review (PM)

- PM reviews package completeness and technical adequacy.
- PM validates scope alignment against contract and approved changes.
- PM returns deficiencies to PE for correction as needed.

### Step 4 - Field Validation (Master Chief)

- Master Chief confirms field execution status aligns with submitted as-builts and turnover scope.
- Superintendent confirms final field punch and readiness items are closed.

### Step 5 - Digital Sign-Off and Release

- PM applies required digital sign-off in Procore.
- No external release occurs before digital sign-off is recorded.
- PE issues final transmittal after PM sign-off.

### Step 6 - Archive and Reporting

- PE archives closeout package in defined project repository.
- PM reports closeout completion in Monday 9:00 AM operations meeting.

## 6. Controls and Thresholds

- Required closeout elements are non-negotiable for release.
- Missing mandatory artifacts block closeout release.
- Digital sign-off in Procore is required before any external closeout transmission.
- Exception approvals require XO review and documented rationale.

## 7. KPI and Review Cadence

Weekly (Monday 9:00 AM control meeting):

- Open closeout items by project
- Closeout package completeness percentage
- Aging of unresolved closeout deficiencies
- Pending digital sign-off count

Quarterly audit:

- Closeout compliance rate
- Rework rate due to incomplete closeout packages
- Closeout cycle-time trend

## 8. Evidence and Record Location

Primary evidence:

- Procore closeout logs and digital sign-off records

Supporting evidence:

- Closeout transmittal packets
- Training handoff records
- Weekly meeting minutes and action logs

## 9. Escalation Path

Standard path:

- PE -> PM -> XO

Command exception path:

- CHENG may escalate directly to CO for safety/compliance risk.
- CPCO may escalate directly to CO for strategic risk connected to preconstruction assumptions affecting closeout exposure.

## 10. Non-Compliance Response

- First breach: corrective action notice with due date.
- Repeat breach: PM/XO command review and remediation plan.
- Critical breach: CO notification and temporary release hold authority.
