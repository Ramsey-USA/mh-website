# Change Summary - Packet 0001

## Scope of new content

1. Procore Governance SOP

- Added PE -> PM -> XO project setup and activation gate.
- Added HR/XO admin ownership for access and permissions governance.
- Added PM approval requirement for external RFI/submittal release.
- Added mandatory Procore digital closeout sign-off requirement.

2. Change Orders and Financial Controls SOP

- Added single-change-order threshold logic:
  - PM authority: less than 25,000
  - XO authority: 25,000 to 49,999
  - CO authority: 50,000 and above
- Added SAGE100 synchronization controls and audit-trace requirements.
- Added CPCO consult role for estimate-driven exposure risk.

## Why these changes were made

- Establish command control consistency before broader chapter rollout.
- Prevent unauthorized workflow progression in Procore and financial operations.
- Ensure threshold-driven approvals are clear, auditable, and enforceable.
- Enable immediate review readiness for CHENG and HR.
