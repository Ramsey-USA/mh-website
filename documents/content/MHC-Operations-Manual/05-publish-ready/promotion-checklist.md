# MHC Operations Manual - Publish-Ready Promotion Checklist

Status: Draft v0.1
Purpose: Define the final gate to promote approved manual assets into publish-ready status.

## 1. Promotion Preconditions

- Chapter has completed CHENG -> HR -> XO -> CO approval chain.
- All required appendices are in approved or approved-with-changes status.
- No unresolved high-severity control gaps remain.
- Version identifier and effective date are assigned.

## 2. Required Promotion Artifacts

- Final approved chapter files
- Approval records and timestamps
- Updated RACI and authority references (if changed)
- KPI dictionary updates (if changed)
- Retention/disposal template updates (if changed)

## 3. Final Validation Checks

- Command terminology is consistent across chapters.
- Threshold and escalation language matches doctrine.
- Mandatory Procore digital sign-off requirements remain intact where applicable.
- Weekly Monday 9:00 AM cadence references remain intact where applicable.

## 4. Promotion Sign-Off Record

Template fields:

- Promotion bundle ID
- Included files
- Final reviewer
- Final approver
- Effective date
- Archive reference

## 5. Post-Promotion Controls

- Archive pre-promotion versions.
- Update implementation tracker and index references.
- Log next scheduled review date.
