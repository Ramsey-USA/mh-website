# KPI and Control Impact Statement - Packet 0001

## KPI impacts

Procore Governance SOP impacts:

- RFI aging KPI becomes authoritative through enforced routing and approvals.
- Submittal aging KPI quality improves through PM release checkpoint.
- Governance exception count becomes trackable via permission exception controls.

Change Controls SOP impacts:

- Change-order cycle time KPI becomes threshold-lane segmented.
- Threshold compliance KPI becomes auditable per single change order.
- SAGE100 posting accuracy KPI becomes enforceable with approval-trace requirement.

## Control impacts

- Control maturity increases from implicit workflow norms to explicit command gates.
- Risk of unauthorized release decreases through mandatory PM/XO/CO threshold routing.
- Evidence traceability improves by requiring linked Procore and SAGE100 records.

## Monitoring cadence impact

- Weekly Monday meeting now supports lane-based exceptions review.
- Quarterly audit now includes threshold compliance and posting accuracy checks.
