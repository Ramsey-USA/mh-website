# MHC Operations Manual - Safety and Compliance Governance

Status: Draft v0.1
Command Owner: Safety Officer/CMO (CHENG)
Backup Owner: COO (XO)
Primary Reviewer: Chief Superintendent (Master Chief)
Final Approver: CEO (CO)

## 1. Mission Purpose

Define the command governance framework for safety and compliance execution, quarterly audits, and corrective action discipline aligned with MHC operational standards.

## 2. Command Ownership and Authority

- Safety command authority: CHENG
- Operational enforcement partner: XO
- Field execution enforcement: Master Chief and Superintendents
- Stop-work authority: CHENG
- Final command escalation: CO

## 3. Governance Cadence

Mandatory cadence:

- Weekly operational safety status in Monday 9:00 AM control meeting
- Quarterly safety and compliance audit led by CHENG and/or XO

Audit coverage baseline:

- Active project safety controls
- Corrective action status
- Documentation completeness
- Recurring risk patterns and unresolved findings

## 4. Required Inputs

- Incident and near-miss logs
- Corrective action records
- Toolbox talk and safety communication records
- Site inspection outcomes
- Compliance documentation from active projects

## 5. Required Outputs

- Quarterly audit report with findings and owner assignments
- Corrective action tracking register with due dates
- Command-level risk escalation summary
- Improvement actions for repeated compliance gaps

## 6. SOP Steps

### Step 1 - Weekly Safety Watch

- CHENG reviews active risk signals and open corrective items.
- Master Chief and PM/PE provide project-level safety status updates.
- Immediate hazards are escalated for same-day action.

### Step 2 - Quarterly Audit Execution

- CHENG defines quarterly audit sample and schedule.
- Audit team validates controls, evidence, and closure quality.
- Findings are graded by severity and assigned to accountable owners.

### Step 3 - Corrective Action Governance

- Each finding receives owner, deadline, and verification criteria.
- Overdue corrective actions are escalated in command meetings.
- Repeat finding trends trigger systemic control review.

### Step 4 - Command Reporting

- CHENG briefs XO and CO on material risks and unresolved findings.
- Command decisions are logged with action deadlines and follow-through checks.

## 7. Controls and Thresholds

- High-severity hazards require immediate stop-work authority execution.
- Unresolved critical findings cannot be deferred without XO/CO acknowledgement.
- Quarterly audit completion is mandatory.
- Evidence gaps require remediation plans with named owners.

## 8. KPI and Review Cadence

Weekly:

- Open incidents and near-miss count
- Overdue corrective actions
- Active high-risk exposure count

Quarterly:

- Audit completion rate
- Corrective action closure rate
- Repeat finding frequency
- Time-to-close critical findings

## 9. Evidence and Record Location

Primary evidence:

- Safety incident logs, audit reports, corrective action records

Supporting evidence:

- Weekly meeting minutes
- Site safety review documentation
- Command escalation records

## 10. Escalation Path

Standard path:

- Field/supervisory chain -> CHENG -> XO -> CO

Exception path:

- CHENG may escalate directly to CO for mission-critical safety/compliance exposure.

## 11. Non-Compliance Response

- First breach: corrective direction and deadline assignment.
- Repeat breach: XO/CHENG command review with mandatory remediation plan.
- Critical breach: immediate CO notification and emergency controls response.
