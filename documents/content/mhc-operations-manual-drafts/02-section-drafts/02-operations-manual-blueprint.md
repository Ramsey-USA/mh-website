# MHC Operations Manual - Chapter Blueprint

Status: Draft v0.1
Scope: Internal operations only
Standard Basis: MH branding hybrid with PMBOK governance

## 1. Manual Objectives

- Establish one command-aligned source of truth for operations.
- Define role authority, SOP expectations, and approval gates.
- Standardize measurable controls, audit evidence, and cadence.

## 2. Required Chapter Schema

Every chapter must include the following blocks:

- Mission Purpose
- Command Owner
- Backup Owner (succession fallback)
- Required Inputs
- Required Outputs
- SOP Steps
- Controls and Thresholds
- KPI and Review Cadence
- Evidence and Record Location
- Escalation Path

## 3. Chapter Set (Initial)

1. Command Doctrine and Authority
   - CO/XO/Master Chief/CHENG/CPCO mapping
   - Officer and execution bands
   - Succession rule and exception routing

2. Organization and RACI
   - Named responsibility matrix
   - Formal reporting lines
   - Operational support relationships

3. Procore Governance SOP
   - Project setup by PE
   - PM approval workflow
   - XO go-ahead checkpoint
   - Global admin ownership (HR/XO)
   - RFI/Submittal routing to Architect/Engineer

4. Change Orders and Financial Controls
   - Single change-order thresholds
   - PM authority baseline
   - COO threshold at 25,000
   - CEO threshold at 50,000 and above
   - SAGE100 synchronization checkpoints

5. Estimator Command and Preconstruction Controls
   - CPCO role definition and authority
   - Estimator team reporting model and scaling controls
   - Bid and estimate control gates
   - Direct-to-CO escalation exception criteria

6. SAGE100 Operations SOP
   - GL/AP/AR/Job Cost/Payroll/Purchasing/Inventory workflow
   - Posting controls
   - Reconciliation cadence
   - Advisory role of CFO function

7. HH2 Timekeeping SOP
   - Employee sign-in process
   - Approval and exception handling
   - Payroll handoff controls

8. Outlook and Communications SOP
   - Account provisioning and deprovisioning
   - Role-based mailbox access
   - Internal communication control standards

9. Internal Server Operations SOP
   - System ownership and access control
   - Backup and recovery operations
   - Incident and escalation response

10. Project Execution and Closeout SOP
    - Required closeout package components
    - Digital sign-off requirement in Procore
    - Release readiness checks

11. Safety and Compliance Governance
    - Quarterly audit cadence
    - Safety authority and stop-work doctrine
    - Corrective action tracking

12. KPI, Reporting, and Meeting Cadence
    - Weekly Monday 9:00 AM operations meeting protocol
    - KPI dashboard definitions and owners
    - Quarterly executive rollup package

13. Records Retention and Document Control
    - Retention schedule by document type
    - Version control and archive standards
    - Evidence retention and legal hold process

## 4. Minimum Appendices

- Appendix A: RACI matrix
- Appendix B: Controls, retention, and continuity baseline
- Appendix C: Weekly operations meeting agenda and minutes template
- Appendix D: Approval authority matrix
- Appendix E: KPI dictionary
- Appendix F: Retention schedule and disposal log templates
