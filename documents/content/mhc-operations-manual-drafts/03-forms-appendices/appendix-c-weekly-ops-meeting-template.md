# Appendix C - Weekly Operations Control Meeting Template

Status: Draft v0.1
Cadence: Every Monday, 9:00 AM (mandatory)
Chair: XO

## 1. Attendance

Required:

- XO
- Master Chief
- CHENG
- CPCO
- PMs
- PEs

As needed:

- HR
- IT Specialist

## 2. Standard Agenda (60 minutes)

1. Command priorities and mission objective check (10 min)
2. Safety and compliance status (10 min)
3. Current project execution status by PM/PE (20 min)
4. Change-order and financial control review (10 min)
5. Risks, blockers, and escalation decisions (5 min)
6. Action assignments and deadline confirmation (5 min)

## 3. KPI Snapshot (minimum)

- Safety: incidents, near-misses, corrective action aging
- Schedule: planned vs actual milestone performance
- Quality: open punchlist count and aging
- Cost: approved vs pending change orders
- Closeout: package completion and pending digital sign-offs
