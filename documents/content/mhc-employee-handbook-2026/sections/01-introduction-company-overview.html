Welcome To MH Construction, Inc.

Welcome to the team. This Employee Handbook introduces the services MH Construction, Inc. provides, gives you a general understanding of the policies by which we operate, and outlines the procedures each team member follows to keep our operation smooth, professional, and successful.

Built on Quality, Backed by Trust.

A strong, productive MH Construction, Inc. team is built on strong working relationships grounded in mutual respect and understanding. Our established policies and practices provide the foundation for those relationships. This handbook is prepared to give you general information about the policies, procedures, and benefits of MH Construction, Inc. Contact your supervisor whenever you need more detailed information.

Who We Are

MH Construction, Inc. is a veteran-owned general contractor serving Washington, Oregon, and Idaho. We build lasting value for our Client Partners through disciplined craftsmanship, transparent communication, and a safety-driven culture. Every project we deliver reflects the standard we hold for ourselves: quality work, delivered with integrity, by a team that takes pride in the result.

Company Culture

Every MH Construction, Inc. team member is expected to contribute to a safe and healthy work environment that promotes cooperative working relationships based on mutual respect and understanding. Because each role matters to the overall success of the company, we ask every team member to complete their assigned work skillfully and efficiently. We win as a team. When we communicate openly, hold one another accountable, and put safety and quality first, we deliver the experience our Client Partners expect and earn the trust that keeps our business strong.

How We Are Organized

To maintain clear communication and accountability across our tri-state operations, MH Construction operates under a defined command structure. Field operations are led by Site Safety Commanders (Superintendents) and Field Safety Leads (Foremen), who report to the Chief Superintendent. The Chief Superintendent works congruently with Project Managers to execute the schedule, while Safety Command oversees the MISH program and regulatory compliance. All operations ultimately report through the Chief Operations Officer (COO) to the Owner/ President.

The MISH Program And Your Digital Resources

Safety is our operational baseline. MH Construction operates under the 59-chapter MISH (MH Construction Industrial Safety & Health) Program, which supersedes this handbook on all matters of field safety, hazard mitigation, and incident response. To ensure you always have access to the latest policies, forms, and MISH chapters, MH Construction maintains a digital Safety Dashboard. You can access the dashboard via the company website or by scanning the QR codes located on the physical MISH binders at your job site.
