Our Safety Commitment

Safety is the operational baseline at MH Construction, Inc. Safe work practices and good housekeeping habits are responsibilities each of us shares. We provide our Client Partners and ourselves with a safer environment by keeping areas clear and hazard-free and not taking unnecessary risks. Every team member has both the authority and the obligation to stop work when a condition is unsafe.

The MISH Program And Site Specific Safety Plans (SSSP)

All field operations are governed by the MH Construction Industrial Safety & Health (MISH) Program, our comprehensive 59-chapter safety manual. The MISH supersedes this handbook on all matters of hazard mitigation, fall protection, energy control, and incident response. Before any project begins, a Site Specific Safety Plan (SSSP) is developed by the Project Manager and Safety Command. The SSSP adapts the MISH standards to the unique hazards of that specific location. You are required to review, understand, and sign the SSSP for your assigned project.

OSHA Compliance And Job Hazard Analysis

MH Construction, Inc. follows all relevant WAC/WISHA, OR-OSHA, and Federal OSHA safety and health standards. Each job site will have specific guidelines that must always be adhered to while on site, and the Site Safety Commander (Superintendent) or Field Safety Lead (Foreman) will conduct a daily Job Hazard Analysis (Tailgate/Mission Brief) for the scope of work to be done that day. Team members are expected to attend these briefings, wear all required personal protective equipment, and follow the site-specific safety plan at all times. Reporting Injuries, Incidents, And Near- Misses

If you are injured or become ill on the job, you must immediately report it to your Site Safety Commander and Safety Command so the company can help you obtain appropriate medical treatment. Prompt reporting protects your health, supports an accurate investigation, and ensures your workers' compensation benefits are not delayed or jeopardized. In accordance with MISH 56, MH Construction maintains a strict Non-Retaliation Policy for Near-Miss Reporting. Reporting near-misses and unsafe conditions allows us to correct hazards before an injury occurs. No team member will ever be disciplined for reporting a safety concern or a near-miss in good faith. Details of workers' compensation coverage are provided in the Compensation & Benefits chapter.

Workplace Security And Non-Violence

MH Construction, Inc. is committed to maintaining a workplace that is free of violence. The safety and security of our team members is of the utmost importance. Acts of violence or threats of violence—whether verbal, physical, or implied—will not be tolerated and will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Team members should immediately report any threat, act of violence, or suspicious activity to their supervisor, Safety Command, or the COO so it can be addressed quickly.

Substance-Free Worksites

A safe worksite depends on every team member being fit for duty. Working under the influence of alcohol or controlled substances endangers everyone on site. The company's full expectations, including mandatory screening requirements, are detailed in the Code of Conduct chapter and apply on all job sites and company property.

Inclement Weather, Heat, And Wildfire Smoke

MH Construction, Inc. functions best when all team members are at work, so it is important to report to work as scheduled. If hazardous weather conditions exist, MH Construction, Inc. expects you to use good judgment, and your supervisor will take that into consideration if you do not arrive on time. Your safety while traveling to and from a job site is never worth an unnecessary risk. On active job sites, MH Construction strictly adheres to statespecific regulations regarding extreme heat and wildfire smoke exposure. When temperatures or AQI levels reach regulatory thresholds, the Site Safety Commander will implement mandatory hydration, shade, rest, and respiratory protection protocols as defined in the MISH program.
