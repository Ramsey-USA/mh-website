# MH Enterprise Document Visual Standard

**Version:** 1.0  
**Lifecycle:** Rough Draft | QR Governance Development  
**Visual language:** Frozen MHC-GC.com-aligned enterprise system

## Frozen system rule

QR integration shall not redesign existing MH typography, colors, logos, page hierarchy, headers, footers, covers, tabs, TOCs, forms, or control blocks. QR treatment remains subordinate to Document ID, title, revision, and lifecycle status.

## Approved placement zones

- Manuals and guides: lower-right control area on cover or controlled footer zone, clear of page numbers and revision metadata.
- Blank forms: upper or lower control band that does not reduce fillable space or expand a one-to-two-page form.
- TBTs and field controls: footer control zone visible on a printed field copy.
- External/SDS content: MH wrapper or index only, never over publisher content without express authority.
- Completed records: no automatic current-template QR addition after completion.

## Physical requirements

- Minimum printed symbol size: 0.75 in by 0.75 in for standard office output; 1.0 in by 1.0 in is preferred for field documents.
- Quiet zone: at least four QR modules on every side, with no rule, text, logo, crop mark, or background intrusion.
- Contrast: dark code on a solid white background; no gradients, transparency, or photographic background.
- Clear space: at least 0.125 in beyond the quiet zone from unrelated page elements.
- Label: `VERIFY CURRENT DOCUMENT` or `DOCUMENT CONTROL`, uppercase, restrained, and no smaller than 7 pt.
- Printed revision: displayed adjacent to, or within the same control area as, the QR.
- Grayscale: must retain strong black/white contrast and pass decode testing from the printed proof.
- Print validation: test at 100 percent scale, grayscale, office laser output, and representative field-device cameras.

## Prohibited placements

Do not place a QR over logos, photographs, watermarks, signatures, stamps, fillable fields, legal text, safety warnings, table data, folds, binding edges, punch zones, trim zones, or other codes. Do not distort, round, recolor, crop, or place an icon inside the code unless a separately approved scannability standard permits it.

## QA acceptance

Every required QR must decode to the expected document identity, retain the minimum size and quiet zone, remain legible in grayscale, avoid route collisions, and pass printed proof verification before publication eligibility.
